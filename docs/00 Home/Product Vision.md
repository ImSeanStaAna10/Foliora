# Product Vision

## Purpose

Foliora helps people collect, shape, publish, and improve a credible professional presence without requiring them to build a website from scratch. It combines a reusable professional profile with a visual portfolio builder and a public, shareable portfolio.

## Vision statement

Make it practical for any professional to turn verified experience, skills, work samples, education, and goals into a clear portfolio they control.

## Audience

Foliora serves professionals across industries and career stages: students, graduates, job seekers, career changers, independent workers, consultants, educators, designers, engineers, healthcare and business professionals, and others. The information model and templates must not assume a software portfolio or a single employment model.

## User value

- Maintain professional information in one private workspace.
- Choose which information appears publicly.
- Assemble a portfolio with a full drag-and-drop block editor.
- Preview and publish a responsive public page.
- Learn from privacy-conscious aggregate engagement metrics.
- Receive, moderate, and respond to visitor feedback through clear workflows.
- Optionally use premium AI suggestions to improve supplied material.

## Product principles

1. **User control:** private source information and public presentation are distinct; publication is explicit.
2. **Truthfulness:** suggestions may clarify or rephrase facts but must not invent qualifications, outcomes, or credentials.
3. **Inclusive structure:** templates and blocks support diverse professions and nontraditional career paths.
4. **Progressive disclosure:** start simple and reveal advanced customization when useful.
5. **Privacy by design:** collect the minimum data needed and explain visitor tracking.
6. **Reliability:** a failed edit or publish must not destroy the last published portfolio.
7. **Pragmatic engineering:** start with a modular monolith and add infrastructure only when a demonstrated need exists.

## Success signals (proposed)

Measure activation (profile plus first portfolio), time to first publish, publish completion rate, editor save reliability, returning creator rate, feedback quality, and support burden. Define targets after baseline research; do not fabricate numeric goals before validating them.

## Not the product

Foliora is not initially a social network, hiring marketplace, applicant tracking system, unrestricted website hosting platform, or automated career decision maker. These are out of scope unless a later product decision changes the vision.
